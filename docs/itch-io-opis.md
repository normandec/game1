## 2. Dolg opis (prilepi v polje „Description")

```markdown
## PROTOCOL SOLAR ECLIPSE: Black Vault

**A top-down tactical campaign against HYDRA — five sectors, one living battlefield, and a hidden 3D S.H.I.E.L.D. vault buried behind the north wall.**

You are inserted alone behind enemy lines at **X 6000 / Y 6800**. HYDRA has taken the
continent: five sectors, each with its own weather, garrison doctrine and command
fortress. Clear sector by sector, upgrade between waves, and dismantle an enemy
war machine that reacts to how you actually play.

### The campaign

Five hand-tuned sectors, from the rain-soaked **Fallen Hub** to the ash-choked
**Hydra Nexus**. Each one escalates: more installations, more defenders, heavier
armour and air support. Finish all five and the protocol is complete.

- **A reactive enemy command.** An AI director profiles your mobility, use of cover
  and engagement range, then picks formations — phalanx, turtle, blitz, pincer,
  encircle, ambush — and shifts doctrine as the fight evolves.
- **HYDRA does not teleport.** Crews board transports in the motor pool, follow real
  routes, slow down and dismount near your position. Seal a gate to redirect a column.
  Aircraft start up, taxi, take off, strafe, return and land in their assigned hangar.
- **Searchlights and alarms.** Light cones do real line-of-sight and exposure tests.
  Get caught and the whole network lights up: five vans and thirty troops roll out,
  with a cooldown so it never spams. Break contact for twelve seconds and you vanish.
- **Airborne insertion.** ATLAS cargo planes drop paratroopers, MANTIS twin-rotor
  transports hover and lower troops on visible ropes. Both can be intercepted on the
  way in.
- **A world that breaks.** Explosions damage walls, hangars, crates and hydraulic
  doors. Long walls split into independently destructible sections, so a big enough
  breach becomes a new road. Loot crates for supplies, weapons and single-use AEGIS
  cells.
- **Six field installation types.** Security checkpoints, bioresearch labs, radar
  stations, armoured workshops, power substations and munitions bunkers — each with
  commanders, shield troops, sentries, patrols and a one-time reward for clearing it.
- **Vehicles and aircraft.** Seize vans, gunships, transports and the NIGHTJAR bomber.
  Fuel, hull damage and emergency ejection are all simulated.
- **Secrets.** A hidden S.H.I.E.L.D. vault hides behind the north wall — the in-game
  **Black Vault** intel tab has the exact entrance coordinates. Inside is a full 3D
  hangar with aircraft requisitions, supplies, medical support and the Carter archive.
  Farther north, the Mnemosyne Library holds a three-volume puzzle worth a searchlight
  identity mask.

### Loadout

Pick one of four operative classes (Assault, Gunner, Demolition, Marksman) and one
support package (AEGIS ballistic shield, Hunter drone, or Sky uplink). Every cleared
wave offers a perk choice — damage, fire rate, pierce, crit, lifesteal, incendiary
rounds, EMP rounds and more — so each run builds differently.

### Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD / arrows | Move, drive or fly |
| Mouse | Aim; hold left click to fire |
| F | Interact, enter the vault, seize or leave a vehicle |
| L | Lock or unlock the nearest hydraulic gate |
| C | Burn one AEGIS cell: three seconds of frontal invulnerability |
| G / right click | Frag, gunship rockets or bomber payload |
| Space / Shift | Combat dash (Shift also boosts a driven van) |
| V | Toggle the directional guide to the vault |
| Esc / P | Pause |

Touch controls are supported on phones and tablets; a keyboard and mouse are
recommended.

### Notes

- Runs entirely in the browser — no download, no install, one self-contained HTML file.
- Keyboard + mouse and touch controls; no camera shake.
- All art and audio are generated procedurally in code — no external assets.
- Progress, best score and the local leaderboard are saved in your browser.

**Tip:** the battlefield is big and the enemy is patient. Use walls, cut the power,
let convoys overextend, and never fight a lighting network you can switch off first.
```

## 4. Slovenska različica opisa

```markdown
## PROTOCOL SOLAR ECLIPSE: Black Vault

**Taktični napad od zgoraj skozi pet sektorjev HYDRE — eno živo, uničljivo bojišče in
skriti 3D trezor S.H.I.E.L.D.-a za severnim zidom.**

Sam si vržen za sovražnikove linije na **X 6000 / Y 6800**. HYDRA je prevzela celino:
pet sektorjev, vsak s svojim vremenom, garnizijsko doktrino in poveljniško trdnjavo.
Čisti sektor za sektorjem, med valovi nadgrajuj opremo in razdiraj sovražnikov vojni
stroj, ki se odziva na to, kako dejansko igraš.

### Kampanja

Pet ročno uglašenih sektorjev, od deževnega **Fallen Hub** do pepelnega **Hydra
Nexus**. Vsak stopnjuje: več objektov, več branilcev, težji oklep in letalska podpora.
Koncaj vseh pet in protokol je zaključen.

- **Sovražnik, ki se odziva.** AI direktor te profilira (mobilnost, uporaba zavetja,
  razdalja spopada) in nato izbira formacije — falanga, želva, blitz, klešče, obkolitev,
  zaseda — ter med bojem spreminja doktrino.
- **HYDRA se ne teleportira.** Posadke se vkrcajo v vozila v motornem parku, vozijo po
  pravih poteh, upočasnijo in se izkrcajo blizu tebe. Zakleni vrata in kolona gre drugam.
  Letala zaganjajo motorje, taksirajo, vzletajo, napadajo, se vračajo in pristajajo v
  svojem hangarju.
- **Reflektorji in alarmi.** Svetlobni stožci imajo pravi test vidljivosti in
  izpostavljenosti. Če te ujamejo, se prižge celotna mreža: pet kombijev in trideset
  vojakov krene nate, s hlajenjem, da ne spamja. Dvanajst sekund izven vidnega polja in
  sled izgine.
- **Zračni desant.** Transportna letala ATLAS odvržejo padalce, dvovrotorski MANTIS
  lebdi in spušča vojake po vidnih vrveh. Oboje lahko prestaviš na poti.
- **Svet, ki se lomi.** Eksplozije poškodujejo zidove, hangarje, zaboje in hidravlična
  vrata. Dolgi zidovi se drobijo na neodvisno uničljive odseke, zato dovolj široka
  prebitina postane nova cesta. V zabojih so zaloge, orožja in enkratne AEGIS celice.
- **Šest tipov objektov.** Varnostne kontrolne točke, biolaboratoriji, radarske postaje,
  oklepne delavnice, transformatorske postaje in skladišča streliva — vsak s
  poveljniki, ščitonosci, stražarji, patruljami in enkratno nagrado za čiščenje.
- **Vozila in letala.** Zasedi kombije, jurišne helikopterje, transporterje in bombnik
  NIGHTJAR. Gorivo, poškodbe trupa in zasilni izmet so simulirani.
- **Skrivnosti.** Za severnim zidom se skriva trezor S.H.I.E.L.D.-a — točne koordinate
  vhoda so v zavihku **Black Vault** v igri. V notranjosti je celoten 3D hangar z
  zahtevki za letala, zalogami, medicinsko podporo in Carterjevim arhivom. Še bolj
  severno je knjižnica Mnemosyne s tremi zvezki in uganko, ki prinese masko identitete
  za reflektorje.

### Oprema

Izberi enega od štirih razredov (Assault, Gunner, Demolition, Marksman) in en paket
podpore (AEGIS balistični ščit, lovski dron ali Sky uplink). Po vsakem očiščenem valu
dobiš izbiro nadgradnje — poškodbe, hitrost streljanja, preboj, krit, vampirizem,
zažigalni naboji, EMP naboji in več — zato je vsak poskus drugačen.

### Kontrole

| Tipka | Dejanje |
| --- | --- |
| WASD / puščice | Premik, vožnja ali letenje |
| Miška | Merjenje; levi klik za streljanje |
| F | Interakcija, vstop v trezor, zasedba ali izstop iz vozila |
| L | Zaklep ali odklep najbližjih hidravličnih vrat |
| C | Poraba ene AEGIS celice: tri sekunde neprebojnosti od spredaj |
| G / desni klik | Bomba, rakete ali bombni tovor |
| Space / Shift | Bojni dash (Shift tudi pospeši kombi) |
| V | Vklop/izklop usmerjevalnika do trezorja |
| Esc / P | Pavza |

Na telefonih in tablicah delujejo dotične kontrole; priporočena je tipkovnica in miška.

### Opombe

- Igra teče v brskalniku — brez prenosa in namestitve, ena sama HTML datoteka.
- Kontrole za tipkovnico, miško in dotik; brez tresenja kamere.
- Vsa grafika in zvok sta generirana v kodi — brez zunanjih datotek.
- Napredek, najboljši rezultat in lokalna lestvica se shranijo v brskalniku.

**Nasvet:** bojišče je veliko, sovražnik pa potrpežljiv. Uporabljaj zidove, odreži
elektriko, pusti konvojem, da se preveč raztegnejo, in se ne bojuj z mrežo reflektorjev,
ki jo lahko najprej ugasneš.
```
